How to use this
1. Start every cube solved. Cube scheme assumed: white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, red opposite orange (with white on top and green facing you, red is on the right). This is the standard scheme on almost every modern cube.
2. Hold it exactly as the yellow box says: one center facing you, another on top. Keep it held that way for every move. The face that ends up toward you is the one that goes in the wall.
3. Do the moves left to right. Step through them here with Next to compare your cube against the live picture as you go.
4. Put it in the wall at its row/column, front face out, top up. Row 1 is the top row and column 1 is on the left (as you look at the wall). Build from row 5 upward.
R / L / U / D / F / B turn the Right / Left / Up / Down / Front / Back face a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that face.
X′ (prime) = counter-clockwise quarter turn. X2 = half turn (either direction).
Solid-colour cubes need no moves. Just a solved cube turned the right way.
Every sequence is the shortest possible (half turns count as one move; checked over every way of holding the cube). Only the front face is required to match; the other faces end up however they end up. For the white 1, every white sticker's logo also ends up upright (logos read correctly with white facing you and red on top in the solved cube). Each sequence was also run on a simulated cube to confirm the result.
