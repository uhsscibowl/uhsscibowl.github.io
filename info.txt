The Novice Amalgam SciBowl Tournament in Irvine (NASTI 1) will be held on Saturday, November 28th, 2026 at University High School. Check-in will begin at 9, and we'll end at about 3 (exact times TBD). NASTI 1 is completely free, and lunch will be provided. We want players with less experience to learn, compete, and get to know students from other schools. We hope to expand the competitiveness of our local regional competitions, so more middle- and high-school students in the area can experience the thrill of Science Bowl! Zero prior experience is required.

Amalgam means that students indicate their past Science Bowl experience and their 1-3 best subjects. The night before, Claude will then balance the teams, combining students from different schools. Register by DMing (on Discord) either Ferris Li (ferris.wheel) from Northwood or Timothy Chen (timothyc.) from University.

NASTI 1 is being organized by members of the University and Northwood High School Science Bowl teams. NASTI 1 is a mirror of the Season Commencing Hunter Invitational Scibowl Tournament (SCHIST) and will reuse its questions with permission.
