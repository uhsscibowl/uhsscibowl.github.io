Toss-upMathShort answer

How much prior experience is required?

ANSWER: ZERO (ACCEPT: YOU DON’T NEED TO HAVE PLAYED SCIENCE BOWL BEFORE)

NASTI 1 is an in-person Science Bowl invitational for emerging teams near Irvine, California.

We want players with less experience to learn, compete, and get to know students from other schools. We hope to expand the competitiveness of our local regional competitions, so more middle- and high-school students in the area can experience the thrill of Science Bowl! Zero prior experience is required.
